# User Personas: Facilities Engineers

## Primary Persona: Facilities Engineer

### Role & Responsibilities
- Monitor electrical system health and performance
- Respond to system alerts and anomalies
- Perform maintenance and updates on electrical infrastructure
- Optimize energy consumption and efficiency
- Ensure compliance with safety regulations

### Technical Background
- Familiar with electrical systems and terminology
- Comfortable with technical interfaces
- May have varying levels of software experience
- Needs intuitive, reliable tools for critical operations

### Key Use Cases

#### Real-time Monitoring
- View current electrical system status
- Monitor power consumption and distribution
- Track equipment health and performance
- Receive alerts for critical issues

#### System Updates
- Modify electrical system configurations
- Update equipment parameters
- Schedule maintenance activities
- Document changes and maintenance history

#### Analysis & Reporting
- Analyze historical performance data
- Generate reports for management
- Identify optimization opportunities
- Track energy efficiency metrics

### User Experience Requirements
- **Reliability**: System must be available when needed
- **Clarity**: Clear visualization of electrical systems
- **Efficiency**: Quick access to critical information
- **Safety**: Clear indication of hazardous conditions
- **Mobile Access**: Ability to monitor from various locations
