# Domain Context: Electrical Digital Twin

## Overview
This application is an electrical digital twin system designed for facilities engineers to monitor and update electrical infrastructure.

## Core Concepts

### Digital Twin
A digital twin is a virtual representation of physical electrical systems that mirrors real-time data and allows for simulation, monitoring, and predictive analysis.

### Electrical Infrastructure
The system focuses on electrical systems within facilities, which may include:
- Power distribution networks
- Electrical panels and circuits
- Load monitoring and management
- Energy consumption tracking
- Equipment status and health monitoring

## Key Stakeholders

### Primary Users: Facilities Engineers
- Monitor electrical system performance
- Update system configurations
- Respond to alerts and anomalies
- Perform predictive maintenance
- Optimize energy usage

## Business Value
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Continuous visibility into electrical system health
- **Predictive Maintenance**: Early detection of potential issues
- **Energy Optimization**: Data-driven decisions for efficiency
- **Risk Mitigation**: Proactive identification of safety hazards
- **Operational Efficiency**: Streamlined facility management
